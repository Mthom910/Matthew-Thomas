Executive Summary

Why Victorian showrooms are declining

Consolidated review across national trends, state comparison, Victoria showroom performance, two product-line deep dives, consultant performance and competitor research.

1 · Overall VIC showroom decline

  • Nine of ten Victorian showrooms trend flat-to-negative YoY; Blackburn is the clear exception, improving across nearly every metric.
  • Clearest overall decliners: Hoppers Crossing, Airport West, Coburg, Elsternwick.

2 · Crimsafe-specific decline

  • Four showrooms show Crimsafe order declines since April vs LY: Elsternwick (-25.0%), Toorak (-18.9%), Hoppers Crossing (-18.1%), Coburg (-12.5%).
  • Toorak: quote volume is fine, conversion collapsed (-9.7 pts intra-year) — a closing-rate problem, cause not yet identified.
  • Hoppers Crossing: conversion stable, quote volume fell -9.3% intra-year — a demand/lead problem.

3 · Roller Blinds-specific decline

  • Fitzroy shows the sharpest Roller Blinds decline of any VIC showroom (-9.7 pts YoY) — a category-specific issue, not flagged in the overall decline.
  • Elsternwick (-6.2 pts) and Airport West (-5.2 pts) also decline meaningfully.
  • Roller Blinds is uniquely exposed to a national online DIY category (up to 70% off RRP) — Shutters/Curtains/Crimsafe need professional install and aren't exposed the same way.

4 · Competitor research

  • Competitors near the declining showrooms are narrower specialists: Crimsafe-only resellers, a roller-blind specialist, a value franchise, and online DIY retailers.
  • The driver of Toorak & Hoppers Crossing's Crimsafe decline is an open question again — likely needs a different line of investigation (pricing, staffing, local marketing, service issues).

5 · Consultant performance (VIC)

  • VIC-wide YoY: Orders -1.2%, Conversion +0.2 pts (flat), Sales -2.2%, Unconverted Value -7.1% (an improvement).
  • Adem Himmetoglu and Adam Cook show the steepest conversion declines (-29.4 / -27.9 pts) — worth a direct conversation.
  • Ricardo Barata and Paul Millan are the clearest bright spots.

6 · State context (NSW vs VIC)

  • NSW outperforms VIC across almost every product group and improved YoY while VIC softened — some VIC pressure may be state-specific.
  • Crimsafe is down by nearly the same margin in both states (~-1.0 pt) — not unique to Victoria.
