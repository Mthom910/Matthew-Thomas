Pilot Overview

Participants

  • 9 sales reps from trial kick-off
  • All sales reps from full rollout

Model

  • Up to 40% discount on jobs over $3K
  • Tiered commission on jobs discounted >35%, starting at 7% and tapering to 4%

Objective

  • Test whether a more flexible discount structure on higher-value jobs increases conversion, AOV, and order economics
  • Reallocate resourcing from smaller-AOV orders into larger-AOV orders
